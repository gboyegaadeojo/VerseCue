# VerseCue — leadership summary

2026-11-17. **PROVISIONAL integration plan; P0 feasibility PENDING.** IFC authorized continued documentation, not implementation or live access.

IFC volunteers currently search for Bible references while the pastor preaches. VerseCue aims to reduce that delay: listen to an authorized microphone feed, identify an explicit reference and offer a large candidate card. The volunteer confirms before ProPresenter changes. Deliberately enabled Auto mode is optional and must stop when uncertain; an emergency stop remains immediately available.

ProPresenter stays in charge of Bible text, themes, room/livestream Looks and output. VerseCue will not become a presentation suite or send graphics directly to screens. If VerseCue fails, volunteers continue ordinary ProPresenter operation.

IFC reports ProPresenter 21 on a Mac mini M4, MOTU M4 audio, English speech, Companion, multiple existing Looks/themes/macros and six installed translations. Same-computer operation comes first; Windows/macOS installation and a LAN companion remain required. The default translation and exact installed configuration still need verification.

The critical uncertainty is whether a supported mechanism can load an arbitrary newly spoken passage while retaining IFC's Bible appearance. Public presentation-trigger documentation does not prove Bible-reference lookup. Saved Bible presentations are an investigation baseline, not a proven solution for unscripted references. No installed hardware or output tests have been performed. The project must not proceed to production code until feasibility and requirements gates are satisfied.

Success requires correct reference/translation, unchanged IFC appearance on both outputs, uninterrupted media/manual control and safe failure. IFC accepted proposed Auto timing targets of median ≤2 seconds and 95th percentile ≤4 seconds; these remain unmeasured. Proposed recognition gates are ≥95% reference precision, ≥90% explicit-reference recall and zero unapproved wrong displays in a 10-hour rehearsal set. These require representative real recordings and do not guarantee zero future errors. Volunteer orientation should take no more than five minutes after setup.

IFC requires free operation. Local speech recognition is the recommended investigation path with no metered speech charge. Existing equipment/licenses, development effort, electricity, maintenance and signing/distribution are separate costs. No purchase, subscription or provider account is authorized. Preliminary effort and cost formulas are in the roadmap; no fixed-price commitment is justified before P0.

Top risks: unresolved arbitrary Bible lookup; incorrect/stale references; interference with Companion/operator/media; local speech load and untested accents; translation rights and signed distribution expenses. The planned next step is an explicitly authorized isolated version 21 documentation/test session, with real sermon excerpts supplied with consent. All remaining decisions remain reviewable before implementation.

Name remains provisional. Five alternatives: PassageCue, ScriptureCue, VerseRelay, PassageAssist and ReferenceCue. Recommend **PassageCue** among alternatives for its broader passage/range meaning; retain VerseCue for this package until IFC chooses. No trademark/domain availability investigation performed.

Audit: covers business case, measurable success, costs/risks, scope and approval boundary without presenting research hypotheses as proven operation.
