## Operator workflow

Enter or detect a Bible reference, prepare it, review the exact saved passage and its preview, then explicitly Confirm. Internal identities and fingerprints select ordered cues; Library/Presentation/Cue fields remain advanced fallback only. Duplicate saved copies require a deliberate choice. Current native saves, locally recorded native copies and unverified existing presentations have distinct origin labels; origin is not text/format verification.

Settings / Output separately enables detected-reference saved lookup or consented missing-passage preparation. Native focus/copy effects are explicit, window/target-bound and never authorize output. Durable copy-accounting initializes at startup, uncertain reservations require inspection, clipping warnings survive reuse, and reconciliation is bounded/read-only. Nothing deletes a ProPresenter copy or repairs formatting automatically.

Confirm remains deliberate; continuation advances only through the exact reviewed passage after prior identity/text readback. Manual takeover blocks further output. Normal Auto approval is available only for eligible reviewed single-cue KJV passages with explicit acknowledgments. Approval sends nothing; Auto policy and audio age/owner/appearance guards remain. Closing/reloading the owning window cancels preparation/input and revokes its grants. Native preparation may change ProPresenter focus and remains restricted to its verified personal Windows profile; unavailable routes explain the limitation before consent.

Original capture age/loss and bounded adjacent citation assembly reach the desktop consumer. Loss resets context; assembled and quoted matches remain Confirm-only. The private API uses the same coordinator through a revocable hook, without remote focus/copy consent or passage confirmation. No new presentation engine, cloud recognition or Bible-text substitution is added.
