## Staffing and estimate assumptions
One experienced cross-platform desktop/integration engineer, part-time QA/operator/UX/security/licensing help, access to both OSs and consented recordings, supportable documented P0 route, no custom driver or reverse-engineered protocol. Effort is engineering person-hours including testing/docs, not calendar duration or paid rate. IFC volunteer time and approval/wait time separate. If UI automation is the only route, estimates are invalid and must be replaced after explicit route acceptance. No benefit claimed from parallel agents or automatic implementation.

For cost review use actual agreed CAD rate R/hour: development cash range = hours × R. Illustrative arithmetic only at a planning allowance CAD 75/hour (not a quote or selected contractor): 40–100 h = CAD 3,000–7,500. Volunteer time may have zero invoice but is not zero effort. No expenditure implied. Capital hardware H and signing S remain quoted/approved separately; current existing M4/M4 interface/Bible licenses are assumed reused, no depreciation estimate. Contingency: add 25–50% to post-P0 effort if unknown packaging/performance tasks persist, explicitly approved before commitment.

## Thin-slice release discipline
MVP includes guarded Auto, default Confirm, Pause/Suggest-only/emergency stop, proven PP link, safe settings, diagnostics and both OS installation as specified. Optional history/manual search can fast-follow v1; advanced context/range requests must fail safely in MVP. If Auto cannot be safe with Companion or arbitrary lookup cannot be proved, present scope alternatives and obtain IFC decision rather than quietly relabelling a reduced prototype MVP. Full requirement/test tables remain even for later features.
