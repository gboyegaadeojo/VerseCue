## Working safely

Use small reviewable PRs and relevant verification. Preserve the original specification and all acceptance requirements. ProPresenter owns Bible text, themes, Looks, playlists and output.

Keep actual host telemetry, audio and private evidence ignored. Routine personal testing has documented boundaries in the active handoff; do not infer church production authorization. No paid services, blind uncertain-output retry, Clear/deletion, automatic restoration or security/privacy changes. Another agent must use its own valid access and runtime permissions.

Private assets, test reports and worktrees are absent from a fresh clone. Do not claim old tests ran locally or start duplicate jobs from stale notes. Consult the active handoff for authoritative branches, exact tested source and any process handles confirmed live.
